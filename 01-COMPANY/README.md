# Company

This is the canonical home for company-wide truth: identity, purpose, operating facts, goals, history, team, approved decisions, and meaningful activity.

Starter records intentionally contain placeholders only. During setup, Codex should draft from supplied evidence, cite the source, flag uncertainty, and request approval for consequential conclusions.

- `company-profile.md` — stable company identity and operating facts
- `goals/` — future outcomes and their status
- `history/` — durable timeline and context
- `team/` — shared team profiles
- `decisions/` — approved decision records and index
- `activity/` — concise, meaningful activity by month
