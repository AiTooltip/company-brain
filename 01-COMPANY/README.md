# Company

This is the canonical home for company-wide truth: identity, audience, strategy, goals, history, team, approved decisions, and meaningful activity.

Starter records intentionally contain placeholders only. During setup, Codex should draft from supplied evidence, cite the source, flag uncertainty, and request approval for consequential conclusions.

- `COMPANY.md` — company identity, purpose, business model, and governance
- `audience.md` — primary audiences, needs, evidence, and status
- `strategy.md` — approved strategic direction, choices, constraints, and links to decisions
- `goals/` — future outcomes and their status
- `history/` — durable timeline and context
- `team/` — shared team profiles
- `decisions/` — approved decision records and index
- `activity/` — concise, meaningful activity by month

Keep each fact in one canonical home. For example, `strategy.md` should link to the primary audience in `audience.md`, while brand positioning should live in `03-BRAND/positioning.md` and reference both rather than repeat them.
