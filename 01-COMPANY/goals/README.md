# Goals

Store durable future outcomes here. Create one file per goal from `11-SYSTEM/templates/goal.md`. A goal states its owner, department, horizon, success measure, status, dependencies, related projects, and supporting approved decisions.

Do not present ambitions found in old notes as current approved goals. Mark them `proposed`, `unverified`, or `superseded` until a person confirms their current status.

Goals connect intended direction to execution and memory:

`GOALS → PROJECTS → DECISIONS → ACTIVITY`

Not every project needs a new decision, and not every action belongs in the activity log. Use links to make the meaningful chain traceable.
