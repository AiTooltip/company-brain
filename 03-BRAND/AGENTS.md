# Brand Instructions

Apply the root `AGENTS.md` first. Brand rules are company-owned and must not be overwritten by framework updates.

Before editing, inspect current brand records, active brand decisions, relevant source assets, and provenance. Separate observed patterns from approved standards. A repeated visual treatment is not automatically a brand rule.

Mark new strategic positioning, identity, naming, voice, visual-system, or public-message direction as `proposed` until explicitly approved. When guidance changes, preserve the old rule as history or mark it `superseded`, link the approving decision, and identify affected assets, products, and projects.
