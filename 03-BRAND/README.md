# Brand

Canonical home for approved brand truth. Keep information deterministic:

- `BRAND.md` for the core brand idea, principles, and distinctive assets;
- `positioning.md` for the approved brand position and market frame;
- `guidelines/` for complete or medium-specific guidance;
- `voice/` for verbal identity, messaging, terminology, and examples;
- `visual/` for visual identity, tokens, typography, color, imagery, and design-system links.

Creative execution guidance may be distilled into `02-DEPARTMENTS/Creative/skills/brand-specific`, but canonical facts should link back here. Company identity belongs in `01-COMPANY/COMPANY.md`, and the primary audience belongs in `01-COMPANY/audience.md`. Do not duplicate them or infer rules from one old output.
