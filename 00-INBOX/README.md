# Inbox

Drop unsorted company material here before asking Codex to **Process the inbox** or **Set up my company brain**.

Good inputs include documents, notes, exports, images, presentations, spreadsheets, project folders, research, and brand material. Do not add secrets or credentials.

Inbox content is evidence, not automatically approved company truth. Codex will inventory it, extract useful claims with provenance, identify conflicts and uncertainty, and propose destinations. Originals stay here until a person explicitly approves moving or removing them.

For an older `AI-DESIGN-SKILLS` or `codex-design-studio` setup, use `00-MIGRATE-OLD-SETUP` instead.
