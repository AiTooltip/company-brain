# Research Instructions

Follow `11-SYSTEM/OPERATING-SYSTEM.md` first.

- Define the question and decision it may inform before collecting evidence.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources; record access or publication dates when relevant.
- Separate quoted/source claims, observations, analysis, inference, recommendations, and approved action.
- Actively look for disconfirming evidence and material limitations.
- Never fabricate a source, citation, participant, result, date, or confidence level.
- Treat research recommendations as `proposed`; only a person can approve the resulting company decision.
