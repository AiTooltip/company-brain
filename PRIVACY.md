# Privacy

The Company Brain stores its persistent knowledge in files controlled by the user. This provides ownership, portability, inspectability, and control over the durable knowledge base, with less dependence on an AI provider's chat history or persistent memory.

It does **not** mean that AI processing is automatically private or offline.

## When AI works with these files

When you ask Codex, Claude, or another cloud AI service to read or work with Company Brain files, relevant file contents, instructions, prompts, and surrounding context may be sent to that provider for processing. The provider may be able to access and process that information according to its product terms, privacy policy, account settings, retention controls, and organization configuration.

Local file storage does not prove that:

- an AI provider cannot access the information;
- everything remains on your computer;
- prompts or file excerpts are never retained;
- a provider's training, logging, security, or retention settings match your company's requirements.

Review the current terms and settings of every AI provider you use. Codex Desktop is the primary tested V1 interface, but provider behavior is outside this repository's control and may change.

## Practical safeguards

- Keep the actual filled Company Brain local or in a private team repository.
- Do not create a public fork containing confidential company information.
- Share only the files and minimum context needed for a task.
- Do not store passwords, tokens, private keys, authentication state, or unnecessary sensitive personal data here.
- Follow your company's confidentiality, data-classification, client, regulatory, and provider-approval policies.
- Check provider privacy, retention, regional-processing, and enterprise controls before submitting sensitive material.
- Review changes before committing or pushing them.

## What “portable” means

The files remain usable independently of one chat thread and can be moved, backed up, versioned, inspected, or used with another compatible tool. Portability describes control of the persistent knowledge base; it is not a guarantee that every AI tool supports the architecture or processes it locally.
