# Build the Core Brand

**Outcome:** establish or improve canonical core brand truth and, when approved, activate a `core-brand` creative skill.

1. Read the operating system, Creative `AGENTS.md` and `DEPARTMENT.md`, `01-COMPANY/COMPANY.md`, `audience.md`, `strategy.md`, relevant goals/research/decisions, and current `03-BRAND` records.
2. Inventory approved brand material, references, examples, dislikes, existing outputs, and explicit preferences. Treat observed patterns as evidence, not rules.
3. Identify confirmed facts, unresolved conflicts, missing inputs, and proposed brand principles. Do not duplicate company identity or audience inside brand files.
4. Present a compact core-brand proposal: idea, principles, distinctive assets, non-negotiables, examples, dislikes, implications, sources, and unresolved choices.
5. Ask for explicit approval of consequential brand direction. Do not infer approval from positive casual feedback.
6. After approval, update `03-BRAND/BRAND.md` and related canonical visual/voice guidance. Record the approving decision when the direction is durable and consequential.
7. Create `skills/brand-specific/core-brand/SKILL.md` from `SKILL.template.md` only when the human also approves activating the distilled execution guidance. Use valid skill front matter and preserve sources, version, approval, and links to canonical brand files.
8. Validate links and record one meaningful activity entry if core brand truth materially changed.
