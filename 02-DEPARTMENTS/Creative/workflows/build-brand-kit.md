# Build a Brand Kit

**Outcome:** produce a reviewable, editable brand kit grounded in approved canonical brand truth.

1. Read canonical company, audience, brand, positioning, voice, visual, guideline, decision, and relevant Creative skill files.
2. Inventory existing logo sources, colors, typography, photography/illustration, licenses, references, examples, and dislikes. Flag missing rights, editable sources, or approval.
3. Propose the kit scope and formats before generating consequential identity work.
4. The kit may include brand guidelines, a brand guide PDF, logo assets, approved colors, typography, supporting visual assets, and appropriate editable sources.
5. Keep editable/source assets in `09-ASSETS/source`, review iterations in `09-ASSETS/working`, reusable approved assets in `09-ASSETS/approved`, and deliverables such as the guide PDF in `10-OUTPUTS`, linked from the owning project.
6. Do not mark polished output approved. Present it for explicit human review and capture restrictions, licenses, and intended uses.
7. After approval, update canonical brand guidance and approved-asset status only within the approved scope. Create a decision record if durable brand direction changed.
8. Verify editable files, export quality, accessibility, color/font dependencies, links, and third-party notices. Log a major approval or delivery once.
