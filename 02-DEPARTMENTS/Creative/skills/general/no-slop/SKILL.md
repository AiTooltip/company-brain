# No Slop

Use this for creative work that must feel intentional rather than assembled from fashionable defaults.

## Before making

Identify the audience, job to be done, medium, constraints, approved brand guidance, and one clear communication priority. If these are missing, derive only reversible working assumptions and label them.

## Quality principles

- Make each visible choice serve the message, user, brand, or medium.
- Establish one dominant idea and a clear hierarchy; do not make every element compete.
- Prefer a small coherent system over unrelated effects, colors, type treatments, and components.
- Use real supplied content when available. Do not hide weak thinking behind placeholder copy.
- Avoid generic AI motifs, gratuitous gradients, glass effects, random pills, decorative grids, empty hero language, and excessive animation unless the context supports them.
- Preserve useful asymmetry and character without sacrificing clarity or accessibility.
- Remove elements that cannot justify their presence.

## Final pass

Check concept specificity, content accuracy, hierarchy, spacing rhythm, alignment, contrast, typography, states, edge cases, and medium-specific output quality. Compare the result against the brief and approved brand truth, not against generic trend references. State any unresolved gap instead of polishing over it.
